## Tour Types

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Interactive Walking Tours" icon="fa-mobile-screen-button">
    Designed for leisurely strolling, these tours incorporate interactive in-app videos and on-demand notifications, while ensuring you have plenty of hands-free time to immerse in the sights!
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Audio-Only Walking Tours" icon="fa-person-walking">
    All walking tours are 75-90 minutes in length and include guided navigation cues so you don't have to worry about continuously checking your phone to make sure you haven't missed a turn!
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Audio-Only Biking Tours" icon="fa-bicycle">
    Many walking tours are also available as biking tours! Biking tours are only available in parts of cities where there are dedicated bike lanes. Safety first!
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
